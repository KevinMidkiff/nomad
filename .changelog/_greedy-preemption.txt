```release-note:feature
scheduler: Add greedy preemption mode (`PreemptionConfig.GreedyPreemptionEnabled`) that masks allocs whose job has `meta.greedy="true"` from placement scoring for non-greedy incoming service- and batch-type allocs (system and sysbatch placements are out of scope and continue to use upstream behavior). The bin-packer picks the best-fit node as if greedy allocs weren't there, then evicts only the specific greedy allocs whose device IDs, ports, or reserved cores the new alloc actually claimed — greedy presence has zero impact on scoring and non-conflicting greedy allocs are not disturbed. Suppresses the auto follow-up preemption eval for greedy jobs so external schedulers can place them without racing Nomad.
```
